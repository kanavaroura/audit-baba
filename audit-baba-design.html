UX Rules baked in

Why this works at the shelf.

One SKU at a time

Big single-item card replaces a 213-row spreadsheet. Counter buttons are 56×56pt — tappable with shelf-dust on fingers.

Variance shown live

As soon as physical_qty is entered, the red callout shows ₹ shortfall. Witness can challenge the number before submit, not next day.

Two-party sign-off built in

Both signatures on the same screen — no "I'll re-key it tomorrow" gap. PDF summary auto-emails to both.

Mobile-first, not mobile-also

Designed for 375px holding-the-phone-with-pickle-fingers. Bottom CTA always reachable with thumb. Numeric keyboard on count.

Heat map separates signal from noise

If a SKU is short across all outlets → POS issue, not theft. The grid makes that obvious in 2 seconds, not 2 hours of Excel.

A11y & performance honest

4.5:1 contrast on Tamarind on Papad White, 44pt+ targets, focus rings, prefers-reduced-motion respected, tabular numerals on every count.
